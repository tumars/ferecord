Huahai · Privacy Policy

For the Huahai website and iOS app · Updated 19 November 2026

Personal information

Huahai has no accounts, does not sell usage data and does not use advertising tracking. Usage analytics is off by default on the website. iOS versions with analytics ask for your permission on first launch; no analytics is initialized or sent before you choose. Choosing “Not now” or dismissing the panel saves analytics as off, without repeated prompts. You can change your choice in My > Settings > Usage analytics. When enabled, we send feature events such as launches, artwork views, close-looking and successful purchases or restorations, together with a random installation or browser identifier, version, platform and language, to Mixpanel (US storage). We do not send names, email, saved lists, search text or purchase receipts, and do not record your screen. The random identifier is persistent, not fully anonymous.

You can turn analytics off at any time to stop collection and clear the local analytics identifier and pending events. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Previously sent data is not automatically deleted; contact the author for help. IP geolocation is disabled, but the receiving service still sees the network connection IP. See Mixpanel’s privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Your favourites, reading progress, page position, daily free close-looking choices, language choice and appearance preferences stay on your device, in browser storage or the app’s own storage. They are not sent to us or third parties. Clearing browser data or deleting the app removes this information. The iOS app’s language is managed through iOS Settings.

Network access

The app includes text and artwork images for offline use. After launch, it checks for content updates in the background and downloads updated text and images when available. Viewing or downloading an original image also uses the network. Opening a museum website, an image source or another external link visits that service, which has its own privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Apple handles Huahai Supporter purchases, restoration and refunds. Huahai does not receive your Apple Account or payment details. It reads the purchase status supplied by Apple on your device to unlock content.

Children

Huahai does not collect information from children. Some artworks contain artistic nudity.
